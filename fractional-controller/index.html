Frequently asked questions

Important details before you begin.

Is a fractional controller the same as a CFO?

No. Controller work centers on accounting accuracy, close, controls and reporting. CFO services generally extend further into capital strategy and enterprise planning.

Can the service work alongside an internal bookkeeper?

Yes. ClarityGrid can review work, establish close standards and provide escalation support.
