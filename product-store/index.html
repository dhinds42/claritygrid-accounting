Frequently asked questions

Are these products accounting software?

No. They are configurable workbooks, dashboards, guides and operating resources.

Do they automatically connect to QuickBooks, ADP, banks or other platforms?

No live connections are included. Future integrations depend on available APIs, authorization, security requirements and separate implementation.

Is customized implementation included?

No. Implementation begins at $2,500 and is quoted separately in addition to the applicable license.

Do the systems provide tax, legal or regulatory advice?

No. Customers remain responsible for their records, decisions, configuration and appropriate professional review.
