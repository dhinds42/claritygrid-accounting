Frequently asked questions

Important details before you begin.

Is a lease abstract legal advice?

No. An abstract is an administrative summary and does not interpret legal rights or replace review by qualified legal counsel.

Can amendments be included?

Yes. Provide the executed lease and all amendments so the current terms can be summarized in sequence.
