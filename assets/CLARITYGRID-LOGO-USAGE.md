# ClarityGrid Accounting & Advisory — Official Logo

## Approved files

- `claritygrid-logo-primary.svg` — primary horizontal logo for white or light backgrounds
- `claritygrid-logo-primary.png` — high-resolution raster version for documents and common applications
- `claritygrid-logo-reverse.svg` — reverse horizontal logo for navy or dark backgrounds
- `claritygrid-logo-reverse.png` — high-resolution reverse raster version
- `claritygrid-mark.svg` — square CG monogram for favicons, profile images and small placements
- `claritygrid-mark.png` — high-resolution raster monogram

## Official colors

- ClarityGrid Navy: `#0B2239`
- ClarityGrid Gold: `#D8A84E`
- Slate: `#566473`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

## Usage

- Use the primary logo on white, cream or light-gray backgrounds.
- Use the reverse logo on navy or other dark backgrounds.
- Use the monogram only where the full name would be too small to read.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least one-half the height of the CG monogram.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, crop, add effects or rearrange the logo elements.
- Do not display the horizontal logo below 180 pixels wide in digital use.
- Maintain the full legal/business presentation as “ClarityGrid Accounting & Advisory.”

## Ownership note

These files establish the approved visual identity used by ClarityGrid. Trademark registration, if desired, is a separate legal process and should be reviewed with qualified trademark counsel.
