ClarityGrid Accounting & Advisory

Accounting experience built across clients, systems and operations.

ClarityGrid Accounting & Advisory is an independent accounting-services practice based in Lorain, Ohio and serving clients remotely. The practice is led by Devon Hinds, whose experience spans client accounting services, full-cycle accounting, payroll integration, accounting cleanup, systems implementation and transportation operations.

What shapes the work

ClarityGrid approaches accounting as a connected operating system. Bank activity, payroll, receivables, payables, operational platforms and management reports must agree—not simply exist in separate applications.

Professional experience

  • Multi-client accounting support across recurring and project-based engagements
  • Accounting, payroll, ERP and operational-system implementation and transition experience
  • QuickBooks, advisory, bookkeeping and payroll credentials issued by Intuit and ADP
  • Specialized experience involving transportation, real estate and complex cleanup work

ClarityGrid Accounting & Advisory is not presented as a CPA firm and does not provide audit, attestation, legal or investment services.
